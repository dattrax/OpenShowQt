# M6 async integration follow-up verification

Implementation follow-up to the [post-6.22 review](M6-post-6.22-review-2026-10-31.md).
Historical findings remain unchanged; this document records their closure evidence.

## 6.23a — Standalone retirement reservation

A completed stage makes one supersession decision under the intent lock and
reserves the bounded retirement slot before surrendering ownership. Both live
replacement and superseded-stage retirement use the same checked reservation;
a busy slot retains the held stage and never overwrites a joinable lane.

`testStandaloneClaimReservesRetirement` injects C from a request thread as staged
B becomes claimable while A has a scripted 1500 ms stop. It verifies preserved
retirement ownership, silent B, responsive passes, eventual C and clean shutdown.
`ctest --preset desktop -R '^background_transport_tests$'`: **1/1 PASS, 9.22 s**
with local PipeWire and Vulkan access; the new live-audio case executed.

## 6.23b — Deferred output transaction

Background application returns applied, video-started, deferred or failed; the
transport no longer retries a media-only transition. OutputSession retains the
scene and retries after retirement capacity returns, suppressing redundant
command-only frames while continuing the current video. A transaction backup
keeps the old background status until the same successful present publishes the
new scene; standalone status remains independent. Named media failures retain
the existing explicit colour-fill fallback.

`async_integration_tests` drives the production WorkerController, worker and
PreviewConsumer. A→B→C and A→B→clear hold B's active scene, global revision,
media path and green pixels while the new scene remains pending; B continues
presenting. Eventual replacement/clear and cancellation back to B are verified.
The transport capacity fixtures now retry from their caller, matching ownership.

The new integration fixture passed in 16.34 s; transport passed in 8.98 s and
output status passed in the same targeted run. WorkerController's existing
suite also passed (54.29 s) during this fix. All used local-session access.

## 6.23c — Standalone request generations

Every request, including removal, stamps intent before validation. A validated
probe can arm only its own still-current generation; cancellation is checked
again after file work. Held stages carry the same generation, and their claim,
reservation and publication are ordered against new request receipt. Completed
worker joins stay outside the intent lock, including retries of parked stages.

The transport fixture rejects stale generations directly. The frontend/worker
fixture removes a playing track while one probe, then three queued probes
(including a repeated path), are delayed by 600 ms. Playback stays removed
through their completions. A separate case shuts down during validation.
`background_transport_tests`: **PASS, 8.98 s**; `async_integration_tests`:
**PASS, 27.23 s**, including the retained 6.23b scenarios, with local-session access.

## 6.23d — Completed seek-clock epochs

Video submissions carry a clock epoch. Beginning a coordinated seek invalidates
old deadlines before decoder requests; pacing can retain one frame from the
requested decoder generation while the audio acknowledgement waits. It anchors
and submits only after clock completion releases the next even epoch. Stale
pending/in-flight submissions return their own lease proofs without replacing
the held audience frame. A newer seek abandons an older retained target, and
control interruptions re-anchor the next frame.

The seam fixture checks disposal of obsolete future deadlines, rejection of late
submissions and preservation of the held frame. The frontend/worker fixture plays
an audio/video clip through delayed 300 ms backward and forward seeks, checks
continued frame/position progress, then checks paused scrubbing and resume.
The earlier seek/pause fixture now waits for the asynchronous target picture as
well as the pause. CPU/GPU drain fixtures sample callback counters after joining
the final callback, closing their previous pre-callback sampling race.

Transport **PASS, 8.88 s**; async integration **PASS, 34.70 s**; playback control
**PASS, 11.76 s**; A/V sync **PASS, 7.35 s**. After correcting the GPU fixture's
sampling too, playback queue **PASS, 9.56 s**. These used local Vulkan/PipeWire;
frame/position progress is verified, not acoustic A/V offset or long-run drift.

## 6.23e — Explicit standalone stage outcomes

Superseded, failed and ready stages now take exclusive branches. Retiring a
successful held player cannot fall through to failure because a moved pointer
is empty; an obsolete failed stage also cannot fault a newer intent. Current
failures retain the existing reason/path pairing and notification deduplication.

The transport fixture covers successful supersession, a failed stage superseded
at its claim boundary, and a genuine current failure with one notification.
The frontend fixture observes transient mediaChanged notifications, so a later
healthy poll cannot hide a false error, and separately verifies an injected
current mixer failure reaches the frontend with its track path.
Transport **PASS, 10.13 s**; async integration **PASS, 37.20 s**, retaining all
6.23b–d end-to-end scenarios.
